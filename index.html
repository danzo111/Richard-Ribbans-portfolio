Services

What I can survey for you

One person to call for the fieldwork and the deliverable. Tell me what you need to know about your site and I’ll work out the right way to measure it.

  • GNSS and GPS surveys

    Survey-grade positioning with base and rover receivers, tied to the national coordinate system.

    • Control networks and benchmarks
    • Ground control for drone and LiDAR flights
    • Setting out and as-built checks

    Trimble R4S · RTK and static

  • Topographic and engineering surveys

    Accurate site plans for architects, engineers and developers before design starts and while building is under way.

    • Contour and detail surveys
    • Site and building setting out
    • Volumes, levels and as-built plans

    GNSS · total station · CAD and GIS plans

  • Cadastral and sectional title

    Property boundary work under South African land survey law, from finding a lost beacon to registering a new scheme.

    • Beacon relocation and boundary checks
    • Subdivisions, consolidations and servitudes
    • Sectional title plans

    I’m a registered candidate land surveyor. Cadastral work is done with, and signed off by, a registered Professional Land Surveyor.

  • Drone mapping

    Aerial surveys by a licensed remote pilot, planned around what you need the data to show.

    • Orthophotos and elevation models
    • Stockpile and earthwork volumes
    • Progress and condition records

    DJI Matrice 350 RTK · Phantom 4 Pro · Pix4D · Metashape

  • Ground-penetrating radar

    Drone-borne radar surveys and the processing that turns radargrams into something you can read and measure.

    • Survey design and low-level radar flights
    • Noise reduction and feature extraction
    • Georeferenced subsurface models

    Zond Aero LF · my own processing pipeline

  • 3D scanning, LiDAR and mapping

    Laser scans, LiDAR and photogrammetry turned into point clouds, models and maps other people can pick up and use.

    • Building and structure scans
    • Point cloud registration and cleaning
    • GIS mapping and cartography

    Terrestrial laser scanner · CloudCompare · ArcGIS Pro · QGIS

How it works

From first call to final plan

  1. Tell me about the site

    Where it is, what you need to know and when you need it. A sketch or an erf number is enough to start.

  2. Quote and plan

    I recommend the method that fits the job, whether that’s GNSS, a total station, a drone or a scanner, and send you a quote.

  3. Fieldwork

    I collect the data on site, check it before I leave and keep you posted if anything unexpected turns up.

  4. Deliverables

    You get plans, models or reports in the formats your architect, engineer or conveyancer asked for.

About

Registered, licensed, qualified

I’m Richard, a geomatics engineer based in Raithby in the Western Cape. I trained as a land surveyor at the University of Cape Town, made the Dean’s Merit List three years running, and stayed on for a master’s in geomatics.

That master’s took me to Antarctica, where I planned and flew a drone radar survey of the Fimbul Ice Shelf and built the processing to go with it. I like jobs where the data has to be collected first, and collected properly.
