Services

What I can survey for you

All services

  • GNSS and GPS surveys

    Survey-grade positioning with base and rover receivers, tied to the national coordinate system.

  • Topographic and engineering surveys

    Accurate site plans for architects, engineers and developers before design starts and while building is under way.

  • Cadastral and sectional title

    Property boundary work under South African land survey law, from finding a lost beacon to registering a new scheme.

  • Drone mapping

    Aerial surveys by a licensed remote pilot, planned around what you need the data to show.

  • Ground-penetrating radar

    Drone-borne radar surveys and the processing that turns radargrams into something you can read and measure.

  • 3D scanning, LiDAR and mapping

    Laser scans, LiDAR and photogrammetry turned into point clouds, models and maps other people can pick up and use.
