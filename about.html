About

Registered, licensed, qualified

The person behind the instrument, and the paperwork that says he knows how to use it.

I’m Richard, a geomatics engineer based in Raithby in the Western Cape. I trained as a land surveyor at the University of Cape Town, made the Dean’s Merit List three years running, and stayed on for a master’s in geomatics.

That master’s took me to Antarctica, where I planned and flew a drone radar survey of the Fimbul Ice Shelf and built the processing to go with it. I like jobs where the data has to be collected first, and collected properly.
